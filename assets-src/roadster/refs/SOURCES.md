## masks/

Cut-outs for the fit, made here: `side.png` traced from the side photo (top edge by brightness, wheels and
rocker from the enhanced dark parts), `rear_high.png` traced on Roadster_77 and snapped to its edges by
GrabCut (tools/snapmask.py); `front_q34.png` traced on Roadster-08-End and snapped the same way.

tesla.com/roadster refuses scripted requests and never loaded in Chrome; it was read on 16 October in the desktop app's
browser. It gives no dimensions (no length, width, height, wheelbase or tyre size). It confirms hidden headlights, a
full-length taillight, thin-spoke wheels, a centre touchscreen with two small forward displays, a fixed seat position,
and that its figures are for the Thrust version. Its images are Roadster-01 … 08 on digitalassets.tesla.com
(Roadster-02-Pilot and -03-Carousel-Slide-1 are the side and front photos used here; 05 is a downforce graph without
numbers).
