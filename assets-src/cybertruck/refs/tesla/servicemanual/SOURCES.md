What they establish: each door hangs on two pin hinges (upper, lower), each a body bracket on two studs
(13 mm nuts, 25 Nm) on the hinge pillar and a door leaf on one stud (15 mm nut, 38 Nm), the pin between them,
inside the door's front gap behind the fender; the door is set by gap and flush, not by a published axis. The
glass is set by its bracket bolts and a pitch jackscrew; no drop distance is given.
