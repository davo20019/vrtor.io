# Tesla body structure dimensions

Tesla Cybertruck Collision Repair Manual, Dimensional Specifications:
https://service.tesla.com/docs/Cybertruck/BodyRepair/BodyRepairProcedures/en-us/GUID-C5797770-FD0F-4A21-90C1-FC4E47976C95.html
Saved 2026-10-04. Copyright Tesla. Reference only; not shipped in the app.

All marked dimensions are millimetres, between the centres of the marked holes/fasteners, with stated
+/-3 mm tolerances where shown. They are structural reference points, not trim or glass outlines.
In particular, the 1543/1689/1691 mm widths in Figure 2 must not be treated as the visible pillar width.
The photographs of removed trim in the windshield service procedure establish shape and assembly,
but have no scale bar for an exact trim depth measurement.
