# Altimeter: Alti-2 Altimaster Galaxy (metric), on its hand mount

The analog altimeter most skydivers wear on the back of the hand. Built to match the real one,
without the Alti-2 logo or the "ALTIMASTER GALAXY" wordmark.

## Dial (metric face)

- One revolution = 4000 m. Majors every 500 m (labels 0, 0.5 ... 3.5, integers bigger), minors every 100 m.
- Red sector 0 to ~780 m, yellow ~780 to 1000 m (measured on FOT-5, inside the scale ring).
- "X1000 METRES" upper left. Cream glow face.
- Needle: black pointer, white tail and white hub, dark centre pin.
