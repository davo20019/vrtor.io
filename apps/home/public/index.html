Open a subject in your headset

No app store and nothing to install.

  1. Open the browser. On Quest, it's the globe icon in your app library.
  2. Type the subject's address. For example vrtor.io/mpc for Machu Picchu. Bookmark it so next time is one click.
  3. Press Enter VR. Look around, and point with your controller at anything with a label.
  4. Using Communication? Allow the microphone when asked. The coach listens and gives feedback between takes.
  5. Want company? In Space, Apollo 11, Wright Flyer, Orca, Taj Mahal or Machu Picchu, choose Join others (or press B or Y on the controller) to appear to other visitors. Your mic stays off until you choose Talk, voices fade with distance, and you can mute anyone just for you.
