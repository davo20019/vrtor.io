About the lab

The library links to all 44 guides on Learn English Sounds; its catalog includes regional sounds and a /ju/ sound pattern. The four guided contrasts use American English. Examples use synthesized voices, with a British voice for the three British centering-diphthong guides.

The mouth demonstration is an enlarged, rotatable 3D cutaway with animated transitions for the eight guided sounds. It is simplified and does not measure your mouth. Live voice bars show microphone volume, without pronunciation scoring. Recorded audio stays in this browser tab and is deleted when you change exercise or leave. Practice counts and listening results are saved in this browser; speaking is self-review, without automatic pronunciation scoring.

Headset: point and pull the trigger to use station controls. Squeeze to grab a word, then release it in the gold answer ring. You can also tap a word, then tap the ring. Lesson steps take you to the mouth and speaking stations with a comfort fade. A or X opens the menu. Use the right stick to teleport and turn. Allow the microphone before entering VR if your headset cannot show the permission prompt inside VR.
