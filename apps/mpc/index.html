About this experience

The main walk uses one connected architectural reconstruction of the citadel, published by Ministerio de Cultura Perú. Paths, courts and terraces share one scene. Dimensions, present-day visitor routes and surface appearance are approximate; the reusable stone relief comes from a Main Temple laser scan; its placement, color and mineral grain are illustrative. No photographic textures are used.

Connected model source · CC BY 4.0 · Model credits and changes. Adapted by vrtor; all 32,759 architectural triangles retained.

Learn opens six separate laser-survey closeups with measured stone geometry, original grayscale vertex values and gaps in coverage. Their positions are independently centred. Use Back to walk to return to the connected citadel.

Stone relief: INC Peru, CAST (University of Arkansas), Cotsen Institute (UCLA), CC BY-NC 3.0. Sampled from the Main Temple’s measured geometry; reused across walls without claiming each wall was scanned. Ground vegetation is illustrative 3D geometry, with no photo billboards.

Terrain: Mapzen Terrain Tiles, SRTM/GMTED2010 data courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey. Heights resampled; the local join and surface colors are illustrative.
