About this experience

The main walk uses one connected architectural reconstruction of the citadel, published by Ministerio de Cultura Perú. Paths, courts and terraces share one scene. Dimensions and present-day visitor routes are approximate. Walls are laid stone by stone along each wall at sizes measured on the laser scans: fine coursed ashlar on the Torreón, the Royal Residence and the Intihuatana, large fitted blocks in the Sacred Plaza, roughly coursed stones in mud everywhere else. Their stones, the lawns and the earth floors are photo scans of real granite, grass and packed earth, graded to the colours measured on photographs of the site. The surrounding mountains use satellite imagery.

Connected model source · CC BY 4.0 · Model credits and changes. Adapted by VRtor; all 32,759 architectural triangles retained.

Learn opens six separate laser-survey closeups with measured stone geometry, original grayscale vertex values and gaps in coverage. Their positions are independently centred. Use Back to walk to return to the connected citadel.
