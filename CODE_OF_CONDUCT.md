# Code of conduct

vrtor.io follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, give and accept feedback graciously, and focus on what is best
for the people learning with vrtor. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated, in
issues, pull requests, or in the shared rooms of the live site.

Report problems to **hello@vrtor.io**. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept confidential.
