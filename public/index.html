Open a room in your headset

No app store and nothing to install.

  1. Open the browser. On Quest, it's the globe icon in your app library.
  2. Type the room's address. For example vrtor.io/ac. Bookmark it so next time is one click.
  3. Press Enter VR. Allow the microphone when asked; the coach needs to hear you.
  4. Speak. Feedback arrives between takes, so you can try again straight away.
